askTRACE processes, analyzes, and extracts insights from Northeastern's TRACE (Teacher Rating And Course
Evaluation) survey data using AI and cloud-native technologies.

- Kafka-based processing on Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE), deployed with Helm.
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over the survey data.
- Infrastructure on GCP managed with Terraform; CI with Jenkins.

Built by a team of students (TEAM03) for CSYE 7125 at Northeastern University.
